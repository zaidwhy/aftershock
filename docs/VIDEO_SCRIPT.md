# Demo video

The rules do not require a video, but judges may judge from the text, images and video alone, and every 2025 winner had one. Record about 90 seconds of the live site at 1280x800, with captions or a voice-over that says the lines below in your own words. Upload to YouTube as unlisted and paste the link into Devpost. The 28 Oct recording below predates this flagship swap (it shows Fukrey Returns in India) and is being re-rendered. Recorded on 28 Oct from the live site: `projects/aftershock-team/video/AFTERSHOCK-walkthrough-v2.mp4` (88 seconds, 2560x1600 at 30 fps, every frame a full-resolution screenshot of the 1280-wide layout at 2x, H.264 CRF 12, captions burned in, no audio), following the scenes below. The older 1280x800 tour was deleted.

1. **The problem (0:00-0:10).** Home page. "A show breaks out in one country. Distributors, labels and brands find out after the peak, then plan from gut feel or a chatbot."
2. **The test that shaped the agent (0:10-0:20).** Scorecard, the Qloo-versus-LLM section. "We asked an LLM what each audience loves. It sounded right, but Qloo could not back 71% of what it named. So in our agent the LLM proposes, and Qloo decides."
3. **Plan a launch (0:20-0:50).** Click "Plan a launch". Let the seven steps play: the forecast (Nightwatch: Demons Are Forever, Sweden, 14%), Qloo's kit, Gemini's 20 ideas, the check ("10 confirmed by Qloo", with the 10 it could not back struck through), the cities (Lidingö, Gothenburg, Malmö), the brief. Pause on the rule line. "Every name in this brief is one Qloo confirms for this audience in this country."
4. **The deliverable (0:50-0:60).** Scroll the Launch Brief: where to start, partners to approach, what not to claim. Click "Download brief".
5. **The map (0:60-0:75).** Home, click "Nightwatch: Demons Are Forever" in Follow one title. The map recolours from Denmark into Norway, Sweden and Finland. Click Sweden: 14%, Lidingö, Gothenburg.
6. **Why trust it (0:75-0:90).** Ledger page. "Every forecast is locked in public before the result exists, and graded from 15 November. Any AI agent can call the same tools over MCP."

Check the live numbers before recording: the week's top storms change every Tuesday, and the step text on screen is what the video should say.
